# Vyom X1 5G

**Live site: https://sahilkumars98755-del.github.io/vyom-x1/**

Concept brand site for **Vyom X1 5G** — a sub-₹20,000 5G smartphone positioned against
Realme P4x, iQOO Z10x, Samsung Galaxy M17e, Poco X7 and Redmi 15 5G in the Indian online segment.

## What's here

- `index.html` — the complete brand site. Fully self-contained: every product render is
  embedded as base64 WebP, so the page makes **zero external requests** and has no build step.
- `review/` — a source bundle used by the review process: the page markup with image payloads
  stripped (`page-structure.html`), plus `styles.css`, `app.js` and the extracted `copy.md`.

## Product positioning

- **Price:** ₹18,999 (8+128GB) · ₹19,999 (12+256GB) · ₹17,499 effective with a ₹1,500 bank offer.
- **Model:** penetration pricing — near-zero margin, but never loss-making.
- **Spec sheet:** 6.72" FHD+ AMOLED 120Hz · Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 · 8/12GB LPDDR5X + UFS 3.1 ·
  50MP Sony LYT-600 with OIS + 8MP ultrawide + 32MP selfie · 7000mAh silicon-carbon with 80W ·
  IP68/IP69 · MIL-STD-810H · 4 OS upgrades + 5 years of security updates · near-stock ad-free OS.
- **Why it wins:** rivals in this price band were squeezed by the 2026 memory-chip crisis into
  HD+ LCD, 4–6GB RAM and 15–25W charging. The combination of FHD+ AMOLED, 8GB, a 7000mAh battery
  and 80W in one sub-₹20,000 phone is the differentiator.

## Honest notes

This is a **design and launch exercise**. The product is not shipping, the specifications are
proposed rather than measured, and every product image is a CGI render produced for this concept —
not a photograph. The page states this in its own copy.

## Editing

The site is a single file. To change it, edit `index.html` and push to `main`; GitHub Pages
rebuilds automatically.
